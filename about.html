Our story

Welcome to C&M Innovations!

We are Carter and Madison, a passionate duo dedicated to transforming your construction dreams into reality. With years of planning and a love for building, we're excited to embark on this journey!

At C&M Innovations, we're open to anything that meets your needs. Whether it's a renovation, custom build, or any other project you have in mind, we're here to help.

We're a licensed and insured general contractor based in Russellville, Arkansas, and we take on residential work across the River Valley: custom homes designed around how you live, new construction from your plans, framing for homeowners and other builders, and remodels and additions that turn the house you have into the one you want.

We're family people, and we believe a home is the centerpiece of a family. So we treat your project the way we'd want ours treated: we return calls, we tell you what's happening this week and what's coming next, and we don't consider the job done until you've walked through it with us and you're happy.

Why we do it this way

We've heard too many stories.

We've heard from too many people who had a bad experience with a contractor: calls that went unanswered, jobs that dragged on, work that never got finished. We built this company to be the opposite of that.

Licensed & insured
