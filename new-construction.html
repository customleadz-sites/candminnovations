Ground-up builds, from slab to keys

Some families come to us with a finished set of plans and a piece of land. They don't need a design process — they need a general contractor who will build exactly what's drawn, keep the schedule honest, and be there at the end. That's new construction at C&M Innovations.

We handle it start to finish: site prep and grading, the pad and slab, framing by our own crew, roofing, exterior, mechanicals through our trade partners, and the full interior finish-out. One point of contact the whole way through.

What we build

  • Single-family homes — one and two stories, from a plan book or an architect.
  • Barndominiums — metal-roof, open-span living with wraparound porches, a River Valley favorite.
  • Shops and garages — with or without living quarters, framed to hold up to real use.
  • Pole barns and outbuildings — clean, square, and built to last.
  • Second homes and cabins — on the lake, in the woods, wherever your lot is.

How a new build goes

  • Plans and site visit. Send us the drawings. We walk the land, check access and grade, and note anything the plans didn't anticipate.
  • Estimate. A written number built from the plans and the site, not a per-square-foot guess.
  • Site prep and foundation. Clearing, grading, pad, footings and slab.
  • Framing and dry-in. Our crew frames it, the roof goes on, and the house is closed to weather.
  • Mechanicals and finish. Plumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation, drywall, then floors, cabinets, tile, paint and trim.
  • Walkthrough. Together, room by room.

We'll keep you posted the whole way. If a delivery slips or weather pushes a pour, you'll hear it from us first — not find out by driving past an empty site.

Why it matters who builds your plans

The same set of drawings can turn into a great house or a headache depending on who's holding the hammer. Square framing means doors that close and tile that lines up. A crew that shows up means a schedule you can plan your life around. And a licensed, insured contractor means that if something goes wrong, you're protected.

We're based in Dardanelle and Russellville and build anywhere in the River Valley. Read about the questions to ask before you hire anyone — then ask us all of them.

What affects the cost

Every job is priced on its own. Here's what moves the number.

We don't publish flat prices because no two lots or plans are the same. Your free estimate is built from a real site visit and your actual scope.

01 /

The plans

A simple rectangle frames faster than a house with six roof lines. Complexity shows up in labor.

02 /

Site work

Clearing, grading, rock, and utility runs vary a lot from one lot to the next.

03 /

Foundation type

Slab, crawlspace or a stem wall on a slope — each is priced differently.

04 /

Size & span

Bigger footprints and open spans mean more material and heavier structure.

05 /

Exterior & roof

Metal roofing, stone, board-and-batten, and porch size all move the number.

06 /

Interior finish level

Builder-grade to fully custom — you choose, and we'll price it clearly.

New Construction FAQ

What's the difference between new construction and a custom home?

If you already have a set of plans — from an architect, a plan book, or a previous build — we call that new construction: we build what's drawn. A custom home starts earlier, with a consultation to design the house around you. Not sure which you need? Call us; it's a five-minute conversation.

Do you build barndominiums and shops?

Yes. Metal-roof barndominiums, shop buildings with living quarters, garages and pole barns are a regular part of our work. Take a look at the projects page.

Can you build on my land if it isn't cleared yet?

Yes. Site prep — clearing, grading, the pad — is part of what we handle. We'll look at the lot during the site visit and include it in the estimate.

Do you build spec homes or on your own lots?

Our work is for homeowners and builders on their own land. If you have a lot and a plan, we're the crew to build it.
