Built the way you actually live

A custom home isn't a floor plan off a website with your name on it. It's the kitchen sized for how your family cooks, the porch that faces the right direction for the evening light, and the mudroom you'll be grateful for every muddy football season. That's what we build.

C&M Innovations is a general contractor in Dardanelle and Russellville, and custom homes are the work we love most. We can take a build from bare dirt to move-in day — site prep, foundation, framing, roofing, exterior, interior finish and trim — with one crew responsible for all of it. You'll know who's in charge from day one, and it won't change halfway through.

It starts with a consultation, not a price

Before we price anything, we sit down together. We want to know how many people live in the house, who cooks, whether you host holidays, what you hated about your last home, and what you've been saving pictures of. Then we walk your land — where the drive comes in, where the water goes, where the sun sets.

Only then does the estimate get built. That's how the number ends up being real instead of a guess that grows.

What's included when we say start to finish

  • Site prep and foundation — clearing, grading, pad and slab or footing work, coordinated so the house sits right on the lot.
  • Framing — done by our own crew, which is why our walls are straight and our roof lines are clean. See our framing work.
  • Every trade, scheduled and managed — roofing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation, drywall. You don't chase subs. We do.
  • Exterior — siding, board-and-batten, stone, metal roofing, porches and timber details.
  • Interior finish-out — flooring, cabinets, tile, paint, doors, trim and built-ins, finished by carpenters who care where the seams land.
  • Final walkthrough — we go room by room with you and fix what needs fixing before we hand over the keys.

Why families in the River Valley trust us with their home

We hear the same worry from almost every family we meet: they, or someone they know, paid a contractor who took the money and never finished. We understand why that fear exists. Our answer is simple — we're licensed and insured, we communicate constantly, and we don't leave. The crew that pours your slab is the crew that hangs your last door.

We're family people. We believe a home is the centerpiece of a family, and we build every one like it's going to hold ours. If you'd like to talk to families we've built for, ask us for references.

The custom home process, step by step

  • 1. Reach out. Call or fill out the form. We'll set up a time to meet.
  • 2. Site visit. We walk your lot with you and look at access, grade, drainage and where the house wants to sit.
  • 3. Consultation. We talk through how you live, what you want, and what your budget needs to cover. Plans get made or refined from here.
  • 4. Estimate. A written estimate based on the real scope and the real site.
  • 5. Build. One crew, start to finish, with updates along the way and a phone that gets answered.
  • 6. Walkthrough and keys. Room by room, together.

What affects the cost

Every job is priced on its own. Here's what moves the number.

We don't publish flat prices because no two lots or plans are the same. Your free estimate is built from a real site visit and your actual scope.

01 /

Square footage & stories

More house is more materials and more labor. A second story changes the framing, stairs and structure.

02 /

The lot itself

Slope, rock, drainage, tree clearing and how far utilities have to run all show up in site prep.

03 /

Plan complexity

Vaulted ceilings, arches, big porches and complicated roof lines take more skill and time than a simple gable.

04 /

Finishes

Custom cabinetry, stone, tile and trim details are where two homes of the same size can land far apart.

05 /

Exterior choices

Board-and-batten, stone, metal roofing and timber accents each carry their own material and labor cost.

06 /

Timeline

A rushed schedule can add cost. A realistic one usually saves it.

Custom Homes FAQ

How long does a custom home take to build?

It depends on the size of the house, the site, and how quickly decisions get made — but we'll give you a realistic schedule before we start, and we'll tell you the moment anything changes it. What we won't do is promise a date we can't keep.

Do I need plans before I call you?

No. Plenty of families come to us with a Pinterest board and a piece of land. The consultation is where we turn ideas into a plan. If you already have drawings, even better — bring them.

Can I be involved during the build?

Please be. It's your home. We'll walk the job with you at key points, and you're welcome on site anytime — just let us know so we can make sure it's safe to be there.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

As your general contractor, we coordinate the work with the county and the trades so the build moves through inspections in the right order. We'll tell you exactly what's needed for your lot when we meet.

What if I want to change something mid-build?

It happens on nearly every home. Tell us as early as you can — we'll price the change, explain how it affects the schedule, and get your OK in writing before we do it. No surprises on the final bill.
