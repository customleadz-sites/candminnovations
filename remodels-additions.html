What we take on

  • Kitchens — layout changes, islands, cabinetry, tile, lighting and the wall you've always wanted gone.
  • Bathrooms — walk-in tiled showers, double vanities, and master baths that feel like a hotel.
  • Room additions — bedrooms, offices, in-law suites and second stories, framed into the existing house the right way.

Improve the home you already have

A well-planned remodel or addition can give you the space, layout and finishes you want without the cost and disruption of moving. Whether the goal is a larger kitchen, an updated bathroom, an added bedroom or a covered porch, we design the work around how your home is built and how you use it.

C&M Innovations is a licensed and insured general contractor handling residential remodels and additions across the River Valley. With our own framing crew, we take on the structural work — removing walls, adding rooms, tying in new roof lines — as well as the finish carpentry, cabinetry and trim that complete the project.

One crew, demo to trim

The worst part of most remodels isn't the dust — it's the chasing. The tile guy is waiting on the plumber, the plumber's waiting on the electrician, and you're the one making the calls. We take that off you. As your general contractor, we schedule and manage every trade, and you deal with one phone number.

We'll also be straight with you about what living through the project will look like — which weeks the kitchen is down, when the water's off, how we'll keep dust out of the rest of the house. Fewer surprises, smoother build.

Seamless additions

A good addition doesn't announce itself. The roof line continues, the siding matches, the floor is level from the old room into the new one. That takes real framing skill and some care with the details — which is exactly what our crew does all day on new custom homes. Questions

Remodels & Additions FAQ

Can we live in the house during the remodel?

Usually, yes — especially for additions and single-room remodels. We'll talk through how to keep dust and disruption contained and be honest with you about the stretches when a kitchen or bath will be out of commission.

Do you do small jobs?

We're happy to look at porches, decks, single rooms and finish carpentry. Give us a call and describe it; we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

Will an addition match the existing house?

That's the whole goal. We tie new framing into the old structure properly, match roof lines and siding, and finish it so it looks like it was always there.

How long does a kitchen or bath remodel take?

It depends on the scope — moving walls and plumbing takes longer than swapping cabinets and tile. We'll give you a realistic schedule up front and keep you updated if anything shifts.

Do you handle everything, or do I need to hire other trades?

We handle it. As the general contractor, we coordinate plumbing, electrical and any other trades so you deal with one crew and one phone number.

We'll come look at it, talk through options, and give you a free estimate — with an honest take on what's worth doing.
