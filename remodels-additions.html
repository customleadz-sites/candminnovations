Remodels & Additions FAQ

Can we live in the house during the remodel?

Usually, yes — especially for additions and single-room remodels. We'll talk through how to keep dust and disruption contained and be honest with you about the stretches when a kitchen or bath will be out of commission.

Do you do small jobs?

We're happy to look at porches, decks, single rooms and finish carpentry. Give us a call and describe it; we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

Will an addition match the existing house?

That's the whole goal. We tie new framing into the old structure properly, match roof lines and siding, and finish it so it looks like it was always there.

How long does a kitchen or bath remodel take?

It depends on the scope — moving walls and plumbing takes longer than swapping cabinets and tile. We'll give you a realistic schedule up front and keep you updated if anything shifts.

Do you handle everything, or do I need to hire other trades?

We handle it. As the general contractor, we coordinate plumbing, electrical and any other trades so you deal with one crew and one phone number.

Tell us what you'd change about your house.

We'll come look at it, talk through options, and give you a free estimate — with an honest take on what's worth doing.
