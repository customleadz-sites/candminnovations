The part of the house you'll never see, done right

Nobody compliments the framing at a housewarming. But every door that swings shut on its own, every tile line that runs true, every roof that never leaks — that started with a framing crew that took the time to get it square and plumb. That's us.

C&M Innovations frames every home we build with our own crew, and we frame for other builders too. It's the trade we're proudest of, and the one that separates a house that feels solid from one that doesn't.

What we frame

  • Full house framing — floors, walls and roofs for new homes, from sill plate to ridge.
  • Roof framing and truss setting — conventional stick framing or engineered trusses, including crane-set.
  • Stairs — laid out and cut on site so they land right and feel right underfoot.
  • Arches, vaults and specialty framing — the details that make a custom home custom.
  • Porches and decks — timber-post porches, covered outdoor living, deck additions.
  • Additions and second stories — tying new structure into an existing home the right way.
  • Pole barns and shop buildings — post-frame construction for shops, barns and barndominiums.

For builders and contractors

If you're a general contractor or builder in the River Valley and you need a framing crew that shows up, communicates, and frames to the print — call us. We understand what it costs you when framing runs late or comes out crooked, and we don't do either. Tell us the schedule and we'll tell you honestly whether we can hit it.

For homeowners

Some homeowners hire us for framing only — they'll handle the finish themselves, or they've already lined up other trades. That works. We'll frame it, dry it in, and hand you a square, solid structure to build on. If you'd rather we carry it the whole way, that's our new construction and custom home work.

Either way, you're getting a licensed and insured crew that treats your framing like it's holding up our own family's roof. Because on the homes we build ourselves, it does.

What affects the cost

Every job is priced on its own. Here's what moves the number.

We don't publish flat prices because no two lots or plans are the same. Your free estimate is built from a real site visit and your actual scope.

01 /

Square footage

More floor and wall area means more lumber and more crew hours.

02 /

Roof complexity

A simple gable frames quickly. Hips, valleys, dormers and steep pitches take longer.

03 /

Stories & ceilings

Second stories, vaulted ceilings and open spans add structure and time.

04 /

Specialty work

Arches, curved walls, stair details and timber porches are skilled work.

05 /

Trusses vs. stick

Engineered trusses go up fast but cost more up front; stick framing is the reverse.

06 /

Site & access

Tight lots, slopes and long carries from the lumber drop all factor in.

Questions

Framing FAQ

Do you frame for other builders and contractors?

Yes. If you're a builder or general contractor who needs a reliable framing crew in the River Valley, call us. We show up when we say we will and we frame it square.

What kinds of framing do you do?

Wall, floor and roof framing for homes, additions and second stories; stairs; arched openings and vaulted ceilings; porches and decks; pole barns and shop buildings. If it's stick-framed, we've built it.

Do you set trusses?

Yes — including crane-set trusses on larger homes. We also stick-frame roofs when the plan calls for it.

Can you just frame my house, and I finish the rest?

Absolutely. Plenty of homeowners hire us for framing only and handle the finish work themselves or with other trades. We'll leave you with a dried-in, square structure to build on.

How do I get a framing quote?

Send us the plans through the contact form or call. We'll take a look, visit the site if needed, and get you a number.

Need a framing crew you can count on?

Homeowners and builders alike — send us the plans and the schedule. We'll tell you straight whether we can hit it, and then we will.
